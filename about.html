Who we are

A food company with

Ride Nippy Private Limited is an Indian food manufacturing, processing and trading company dealing in a range of food products for business buyers, retailers, distributors and individual consumers.

Our portfolio includes pickles, vinegar, fruit jam, edible oils, nuts, spices, lentils and pulses. We aim to make product discovery and business enquiries straightforward, with clear communication about product requirements and supply needs.

As our product range and operations develop, this website will be updated with verified company information, product specifications and relevant business credentials.
